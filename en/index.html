    // why comfor

    Composite Forming is complex.
    Your solver should stay transparent.

    Comfor is an open-source explicit FEM solver designed for composite manufacturing research, where constitutive models, input files, and solver choices remain inspectable and reproducible.

    —
    Traceable models.

    Constitutive models are documented and linked to their source publications.

    —
    Composite-first design.

    Dry forming, thermostamping and RTM are treated as native use cases.

    —
    Reproducible workflows.

    Inputs, material parameters and solver settings can be versioned, shared and audited.

    // simulation capabilities

    What Comfor can simulate

    A modular explicit FEM solver purpose-built for composite manufacturing. Each capability is backed by peer-reviewed constitutive models — no empirical shortcuts.

    01

    Manufacturing processes

    Dry forming, thermostamping, draping, RTM, filament winding. Explicit non-linear FEM handles large deformations and transient dynamics throughout the forming cycle.

    02

    Composite material models

    Woven fabrics, UD and NCF composites, anisotropic textile constitutive models, viscoplastic matrices. Every constitutive model links directly to its source publication.

    03

    Results & visualization

    Stresses, displacements, fiber orientations, shear angles, and contact forces. Full ParaView integration for interactive post-processing and animation.
