<div class="lp-why">
  <p class="lp-eyebrow">// why comfor</p>
  <div class="lp-why__layout">
    <div class="lp-why__left">
      <h2 class="lp-section-heading"><span class="lp-why__mark">Com</span>posite <span class="lp-why__mark">For</span>ming is complex.<br>Your solver should stay transparent.</h2>
      <p>Comfor is an open-source explicit FEM solver designed for composite manufacturing research, where constitutive models, input files and solver choices remain inspectable and reproducible.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-why__right">
      <div class="lp-why__item">
        <span class="lp-why__dash">—</span>
        <div><strong>Traceable models.</strong><p>Constitutive models are documented and linked to their source publications.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="lp-why__item">
        <span class="lp-why__dash">—</span>
        <div><strong>Composite-first design.</strong><p>Dry forming, thermostamping and RTM are treated as native use cases.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="lp-why__item">
        <span class="lp-why__dash">—</span>
        <div><strong>Reproducible workflows.</strong><p>Inputs, material parameters and solver settings can be versioned, shared and audited.</p></div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lp-platform">
  <p class="lp-eyebrow">// simulation capabilities</p>
  <h2 class="lp-section-heading">What Comfor can simulate</h2>
  <p class="lp-platform__intro">A modular explicit FEM solver purpose-built for composite manufacturing. Each capability is backed by peer-reviewed constitutive models — no empirical shortcuts.</p>
  <div class="lp-platform__grid">
    <div class="lp-platform__item">
      <span class="lp-pillar__num">01</span>
      <h3>Manufacturing processes</h3>
      <p>Dry forming, thermostamping, draping, RTM, filament winding. Explicit non-linear FEM handles large deformations and transient dynamics throughout the forming cycle.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-platform__item">
      <span class="lp-pillar__num">02</span>
      <h3>Composite material models</h3>
      <p>Woven fabrics, UD and NCF composites, anisotropic textile constitutive models, viscoplastic matrices. Every constitutive model links directly to its source publication.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-platform__item">
      <span class="lp-pillar__num">03</span>
      <h3>Results &amp; visualization</h3>
      <p>Stresses, displacements, fiber orientations, shear angles, contact forces. Full Paraview integration for interactive post-processing and animation.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
